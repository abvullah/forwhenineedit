Accessibility

For when I need it aims to make its pages and file tools usable with keyboards, screen readers, zoom, and reduced-motion preferences. Accessibility is an ongoing effort; this statement is not a claim of conformance or certification.

Known limitations

Some canvas-based image, QR, barcode, and visual comparison tools may not expose every visual operation to assistive technology. Camera scanning depends on browser permission and device support. We are working to improve labels, status announcements, focus order, and keyboard access.

Get help or report a barrier

Describe the page or tool, what you were trying to do, and the assistive technology/browser involved. Avoid sending sensitive files. Add your preferred contact information to the About page when available.
