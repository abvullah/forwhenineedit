About

Inspired by the shutdown of remove.bg, this project aims to keep useful background-removal tools accessible to everyone.

When remove.bg shut down, it left a gap for people who relied on a simple way to remove image backgrounds. For when I need it was created to offer a free alternative that works right in your browser, without paywalls, accounts, or subscriptions.

The goal is to keep practical tools easy to access: open the website, use what you need, and keep your files on your device.

Your files are processed in your browser and are not uploaded to us.
