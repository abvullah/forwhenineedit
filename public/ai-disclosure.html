AI and automated tools

The background-removal tool runs a locally hosted machine-learning model in your browser. The selected image is processed on your device; the model does not send it to a remote AI provider. We do not use files to train models.

Limitations

Automated results can omit details, alter edges, or fail on unusual images. Review the output and use the provided brushes to refine it. Do not rely on automated output for safety-critical, legal, medical, or other consequential decisions.

Other processing

OCR, media conversion, and file conversion use locally hosted software. Processing runs in your browser and requires no file upload. See the Privacy Policy.
